## Detailed Game Mechanics

Keyfury combines deep fighting game architecture with precision typing metrics:

- **1v1 Real-Time Combat Typing**: Players face off in real-time. Typing target words displayed above your stickman warrior executes attack commands instantly.
- **100 HP Health Bar System**: Each fighter enters the ring with a **100 HP health bar**. Successful, accurate target words deal damage proportional to word length and execution speed, draining the opponent's health bar.
- **90s Round Timer**: Rounds run on a strict **90s round timer**. Players can achieve an instant win by delivering a Knockout (KO) within 90 seconds, or win by having higher remaining HP when the 90s timer runs out.
- **Accuracy-Based Combo Multipliers**: Typing consecutive words without miskeys builds a **combo streak**. Maintaining high accuracy triggers **accuracy-based combo multipliers** (scaling damage output up to 1.5x) and launches special visual finishes like Flying Dragon Kicks and Energy Projectiles. Miskeys cause your fighter to stumble, leaving you vulnerable to counter-attacks while correcting typos.
- **5-Second Bot Fallback Matchmaking**: Never wait in long queues. Keyfury's matchmaking engine searches for active human opponents and automatically executes a **5-second bot fallback matchmaking** sequence, seamlessly bringing in dynamic AI Bot Warriors (`Bot_Ninja`, `Bot_Ronin`, `Bot_Viper`) tuned to your skill level.
- **Elo/MMR Rank Tiers**: Track your skill progression across competitive rank tiers utilizing a refined Elo matchmaking rating system:
  - **Bronze Tier** (0 - 999 MMR)
  - **Silver Tier** (1000 - 1399 MMR)
  - **Gold Tier** (1400 - 1799 MMR)
  - **Platinum Tier** (1800 - 2199 MMR)
  - **Diamond Tier** (2200 - 2599 MMR)
  - **Fury Master Tier** (2600+ MMR)
- **Stickman Fighting Visuals**: Enjoy smooth canvas-rendered **stickman fighting visuals**, complete with dynamic martial arts animation sprites, vivid color-coded keystroke highlighting, screen shake impacts, and particle effects.

## 1. Overview & Core Gameplay

KeyFury revolutionizes online **typing games** and **keyboard games** by turning standard typing tests into an adrenaline-fueled 3D fighting arena. Built on Three.js and WebGL, KeyFury transforms every completed word into physical martial arts strikes, katana slashes, titanium gauntlet blows, and lightning finishers.

Whether you are looking to test your WPM in **typing fighting games**, climb competitive Elo rating ladders, or engage in **1v1 combat keyboard games**, KeyFury provides an instant, free-to-play multiplayer action experience.

## 2. Detailed 3D Game Mechanics

KeyFury combines fighting game architecture with precision typing mechanics:

- **1v1 Real-Time 3D Combat Typing**: Players face off in real-time. Typing target words displayed above your champion executes attack commands instantly.
- **200 HP Health Bar System**: Each fighter enters the arena with a **200 HP health bar**. Successful, accurate target words deal damage to drain the opponent's health bar.
  - Short words: Jabs (2 base damage)
  - Medium words: Kicks (3 base damage)
  - Long words: Heavy Attacks (4 base damage)
  - Finishers: Special prompt strikes (6 base damage)
- **Combo Overdrive Multipliers**: Flawless word streaks unlock Power Strike (+1), Weapon Finisher (+2), and Overdrive (+3) bonus damage.
- **-2 HP Error Penalty**: Every miskey immediately inflicts -2 HP damage upon your own fighter, eliminating spamming and rewarding clean touch typing.
- **60-to-90s Round Timer**: Rounds conclude on Knockout (KO) or highest remaining HP at timer expiration.
- **Four Playable 3D Champions**:
  - Shadow Ronin (Kage): Precision Katana Striker, Azure Plasma
  - Cyber Valkyrie (Freya): Heavy Titanium Exo-Brawler, Crimson Core
  - Volt Shinobi (Raijin): High-Voltage Cyber Infiltrator, Volt Lightning
  - Void Assassin (Nyx): Shadow Rift Dimensional Stalker, Amethyst Void
- **Four Dynamic 3D Arenas**: Highland Sanctuary, Cyber Rooftop, Volcanic Caldera, Celestial Void.
- **5-Second Bot Fallback Matchmaking**: Seamless AI bot warriors step in if human matchmaking takes longer than 5 seconds, eliminating queue waits.
- **Elo/MMR Rank Tiers**: Bronze (0-999), Silver (1000-1399), Gold (1400-1799), Platinum (1800-2199), Diamond (2200-2599), Fury Master (2600+).

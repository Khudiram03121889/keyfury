## 1. Game Mechanics Summary

KeyFury (`https://keyfury.in/`) is a free, web-based 3D competitive typing fighting game powered by Three.js and WebGL. It transforms standard typing practice into high-intensity 1v1 martial arts duels.

### Key Combat Features
- **200 HP Health Bar System**: Both fighters start with 200 HP. Land strikes by typing target words accurately to deplete the opponent's health bar.
- **Word Tiers & Damage**:
  - Short words: Fast Jabs (2 base damage)
  - Medium words: Kicks (3 base damage)
  - Long words: Heavy Strikes (4 base damage)
  - Weapon Finishers: Lethal prompt strikes (6 base damage)
- **Combo Overdrive Multipliers**: Flawless word streaks unlock Power Strike (+1), Weapon Finisher (+2), and Overdrive (+3) bonus damage.
- **-2 HP Error Penalty**: Every miskey immediately subtracts 2 HP from your own health bar, rewarding disciplined accuracy over frantic mashing.
- **60-to-90s Round Timer**: Duels last 60 to 90 seconds. Win instantly via Knockout (KO) or hold the higher HP percentage when time expires.
- **Four 3D Cybernetic Champions**: Shadow Ronin (Katana Striker), Cyber Valkyrie (Heavy Exo-Brawler), Volt Shinobi (Lightning Rushdown), and Void Assassin (Dimensional Stalker).
- **Four Dynamic 3D Arenas**: Highland Sanctuary, Cyber Rooftop, Volcanic Caldera, and Celestial Void.
- **5-Second Bot Fallback Matchmaking**: Seamless AI bot warrior integration ensures instant matches at any skill tier without queue delays.
- **Elo/MMR Rank Tiers**: Rise from Bronze to Fury Master rank tier by out-typing opponent fighters in ranked duels.

## 2. Competitive Combat Strategy Tips

1. **Prioritize Accuracy Over Raw WPM**: Because every typing error penalizes you with -2 HP, maintaining 98%+ accuracy yields significantly higher net health differentials than reckless high-WPM mashing.
2. **Harness Combo Overdrive**: Building a 7+ word streak adds +3 damage to every single word typed. A single overdrive flurry can drain 40+ HP from your rival in seconds.
3. **Master Rapid Typo Recovery**: With 0ms input lockout, instant backspace correction minimizes cadence disruption when a mistake occurs.
4. **Choose Champions Suited to Your Rhythm**: Whether you favor the razor precision of Shadow Ronin or the relentless rushdown of Volt Shinobi, match your typing tempo to your fighter's combat style.
5. **Manage the Timer Clock**: If you hold an HP lead under 15 seconds remaining, focus on safe, steady word execution rather than rushing long words.

# Keyfury Game Guide & Strategy Handbook

> Master the art of **1v1 combat keyboard games** and dominate **typing fighting games** with this comprehensive Keyfury strategy guide.

## Game Mechanics Summary

Keyfury (`https://keyfury.in/`) is a free, web-based competitive typing game that transforms standard **typing tests** into real-time stickman fighting duels.

### Key Combat Features
- **100 HP Health Bar System**: Both fighters start each match with 100 HP. Land strikes by typing target words accurately to deplete the opponent's health bar.
- **90s Round Timer**: Duels last 90 seconds. Win instantly via Knockout (KO) or hold the higher HP percentage when the timer expires.
- **Accuracy-Based Combo Multipliers**: Build accuracy streaks to activate up to 1.5x damage multipliers and execute high-flying martial arts special attacks.
- **5-Second Bot Fallback Matchmaking**: Seamless AI bot warrior integration ensures instant matches at any skill level without queue delays.
- **Elo/MMR Rank Tiers**: Rise from Bronze to Fury Master rank tier by out-typing opponent fighters in ranked duels.
- **Stickman Fighting Visuals**: Dynamic martial arts stickman animations bring every typed keystroke to life.

---

## Keyfury Combat Strategy Tips

1. **Prioritize Accuracy Over Raw WPM**: Miskeys cause your stickman fighter to stumble, freezing your attack window. Maintaining 98%+ accuracy yields higher damage output through **accuracy-based combo multipliers**.
2. **Control the 90s Round Timer**: If you hold an HP lead under 15 seconds remaining, focus on flawless word execution rather than rushing risky words.
3. **Master Typo Recovery**: When a miskey occurs, use immediate backspaces. Staying calm under pressure prevents cascading errors during opponent strike strings.
4. **Learn Opponent Rhythms in 1v1 Combat**: Watch whether human or bot opponents spike in speed or stumble on longer words, and capitalize with high-damage combo attacks.

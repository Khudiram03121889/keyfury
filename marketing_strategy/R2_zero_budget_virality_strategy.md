### 1. YouTube Shorts Optimization Engine

YouTube Shorts evaluates a video in two primary stages:

1. **The Seed Test (First 100–500 Impressions):**
   * **Shown in Feed vs. Viewed Ratio:** If 100 people scroll past your Short in their feed, at least **70 to 80 people must choose to stay and watch**. If the viewed ratio drops below 65%, YouTube stops distributing the Short.
   * *Tactical Fix for KeyFury:* The very first frame (0:00) must contain high-contrast movement (stickman landing a kick or dynamic typing text) to capture eyes before the user swiping gesture completes.

2. **The Retention & Loop Scale (500 to 100,000+ Views):**
   * **Relative Retention Rate (Watch Time %):** For a 15-to-25 second Short, YouTube requires a **Watch Time percentage above 100%** (meaning the average viewer watches the Short 1.1x to 1.5x times through a seamless loop).
   * **Short Length Strategy:** Keep KeyFury Shorts between **18 and 28 seconds**. Videos in this length bucket hit the highest rate of 100%+ completion.

---

### 2. Instagram Reels Optimization Engine

Instagram’s algorithm prioritizes **private social interactions** over passive watching:

1. **DM Share Velocity (The #1 Viral Ranking Factor):**
   * Instagram heavily favors videos that get sent in Direct Messages (DMs) to friends. 
   * *Tactical Fix for KeyFury:* Frame content around social challenges and rivalries ("Send this to the friend who types at 40 WPM" or "Tag the friend you want to 1v1 right now").

2. **Save & Re-Watch Metrics:**
   * Saves indicate high value or ASMR satisfaction. ASMR mechanical keyboard typing clips naturally trigger high save rates.

---

### 3. Audio Trend Layering & Original Sound Optimization

* **Original Sound Authority:** Create an official original sound named: `KeyFury - ASMR Mechanical Typing Combat`.
* **Low-Volume Trending Audio Layering:** When posting on IG Reels, attach a trending audio track, but reduce its volume to **5-10%**, while boosting KeyFury’s mechanical typing + hit sound effects to **90-95%**. This allows the algorithm to push the video via the trending audio tag while preserving the satisfying sound design.

---

### 4. Seamless Loop Triggers

To force automatic re-watches, construct scripts where the **ending sentence seamlessly completes the opening sentence**.

* *Opening Sentence (0:00):* "...is why typing tests are officially dead."
* *Ending Sentence (0:29):* "And that..."
* *Full Seamless Loop:* "And that... is why typing tests are officially dead."

---

### 5. Shadowban & Algorithmic Penalty Avoidance

To prevent videos from receiving 0 views or algorithmic throttling:

1. **Zero Watermarks:** Never post clips with TikTok watermarks or CapCut logos on IG Reels or YT Shorts.
2. **Native 9:16 Aspect Ratio:** Always render at **1080x1920 (9:16 vertical format)** at 60 FPS. Never upload letterboxed horizontal video with black bars top and bottom.
3. **Metadata Variety:** Never repeat exact identical titles or hashtag blocks across posts; algorithms flag identical text metadata as automated spam.
4. **Safe CTAs:** Never say "Click the link in my bio" in the voiceover on YouTube Shorts. Instead, say "Try it free at keyfury.in" and put the link in the pinned comment.

---

### 6. Optimal Scheduling & Posting Frequency

* **Frequency:** **2 to 3 posts per day** across both YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (Total: 6 uploads/day).
* **Optimal Post Windows (Target Audience Time Zones - EST/PST/UTC):**
  * **Window 1 (Lunch Peak):** 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM local time.
  * **Window 2 (After School / Work):** 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM local time.
  * **Window 3 (Prime Gaming Peak):** 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM local time.
